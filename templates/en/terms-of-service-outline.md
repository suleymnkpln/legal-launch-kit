# Terms of Service — outline

1. **Parties and acceptance:** who you contract with (legal name, address); how acceptance happens (active click, not pre-ticked).
2. **The service:** what is included; beta/AI features and their limits.
3. **Accounts:** eligibility and age, account security, suspension/termination and how to delete.
4. **Prices and payment:** prices incl. taxes, billing period, **auto-renewal and how to cancel**, trials, refunds.
5. **Consumer rights:** withdrawal right (14 days in TR/EU) and its exceptions; EU withdrawal function; statutory rights not limited by these terms.
6. **User content:** licence you need, prohibited content, **notice-and-takedown / reporting**, repeat-infringer policy.
7. **Acceptable use:** abuse, scraping, security testing.
8. **Intellectual property.**
9. **Third-party services** (app stores, payment providers).
10. **Liability:** limits that are valid under consumer law (do not exclude liability that cannot be excluded).
11. **Changes to the terms:** notice period and channel.
12. **Governing law and disputes:** consumer-protective rules (consumer arbitration committees in TR; ADR info where required in the EU).
13. **Contact.**

Link the Privacy Policy. Do not make users "accept" it; it is a notice.
