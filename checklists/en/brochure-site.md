# Checklist: brochure / corporate site (TR + EU)

No sales, no accounts, at most a contact form. Details and sources: [matrix](../../skills/legal-launch-audit/references/matrix.md). Last verified: 2026-11-17.

## Required
- [ ] **Privacy notice** (KVKK *aydınlatma metni* / GDPR notice): needed if you have a contact form, analytics or server logs with IPs. Link it right next to the form.
- [ ] **Request channel** for data-subject rights (email or form), named in the notice.
- [ ] **Business identity / contact:** name, address, email, phone (+ registry/VAT for commercial sites; *Impressum* in DE/AT).
- [ ] **HTTPS** with an HTTP→HTTPS redirect.
- [ ] **Licensed images, fonts and icons.** Keep the proof.
- [ ] **Data minimisation:** no national ID, birth date or other unneeded fields.

## Conditional
- [ ] **Cookie banner + cookie policy:** only if you run analytics, ads or pixels. "Reject" must be as easy as "accept", and nothing may fire before consent.
- [ ] **International transfers:** Google Analytics, foreign hosting and form SaaS must be disclosed, with safeguards (TR: standard contract + notification within 5 business days).
- [ ] **Newsletter:** separate, unticked opt-in; TR: İYS registration.
- [ ] **AI chatbot:** tell users they are talking to AI (EU AI Act Art. 50).

## Not needed / avoid 🗑️
- A **mandatory "I give explicit consent" checkbox** on the contact form. Replying to a message does not rely on consent.
- Distance-sales contract, pre-contract form, returns policy or ETBİS badge when you sell nothing.
- An EU ODR platform link (closed 2025).
- A cookie banner when no trackers run. Not illegal, just noise.
