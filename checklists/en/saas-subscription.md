# Checklist: SaaS / subscription

Last verified: 2026-11-17. Sources: [matrix](../../skills/legal-launch-audit/references/matrix.md).

- [ ] **Privacy policy** naming every sub-processor (hosting, email, analytics, AI APIs).
- [ ] **Terms of service:** accounts, fees, cancellation, liability, acceptable use.
- [ ] **DPA template + sub-processor list** for B2B customers (GDPR Art. 28).
- [ ] **Subscription disclosure before purchase:** price, period, auto-renewal, how to cancel.
- [ ] **Easy cancellation:** as easy as signing up (US ROSCA + state laws; TR subscription regulation; EU withdrawal button for B2C).
- [ ] **B2C sales:** pre-contract info. For instantly performed digital services, capture express consent + acknowledgement to waive withdrawal.
- [ ] **Account deletion and data export** paths.
- [ ] **International transfers** (GDPR Chapter V; TR KVKK Art. 9).
- [ ] **AI features:** disclose AI interaction and mark generated content (EU AI Act Art. 50).
- [ ] **US:** a privacy policy is always needed (CalOPPA). Above CCPA thresholds: opt-out links + Global Privacy Control.
- [ ] **Cookie consent** if analytics or ads run.

## Avoid 🗑️
- Asking for "consent" to process data you need to deliver the service.
- Making users "accept" the privacy policy. It is a notice, not a contract.
