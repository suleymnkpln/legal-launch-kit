# Checklist: e-commerce (TR + EU)

Selling goods/services to consumers via your own site or app. Sources: [turkiye.md](../../skills/legal-launch-audit/references/turkiye.md), [eu.md](../../skills/legal-launch-audit/references/eu.md). Last verified: 2026-11-17.

## Privacy
- [ ] **Privacy notice** linked at sign-up, checkout and every form.
- [ ] **Consent only where consent is the basis:** marketing and non-essential cookies, as separate unticked boxes.
- [ ] **Cookie banner + policy:** trackers and ads blocked until consent.
- [ ] **Processor contracts (DPAs) and international transfer safeguards** for payment, hosting, email and analytics.

## Commerce
- [ ] **Imprint:** legal name, address, registry no., VAT/MERSİS, email, phone.
- [ ] **TR: ETBİS registration** before selling.
- [ ] **Pre-contract information**, confirmed before the order (TR: since 2026 it includes the mediation notice).
- [ ] **Contract confirmation** on a durable medium (email/PDF).
- [ ] **14-day withdrawal right** with correct exceptions. TR: return shipping cannot be charged to the consumer (2026).
- [ ] **EU withdrawal button** (since 19 June 2026): two-step, with an acknowledgement on a durable medium.
- [ ] **Total price incl. taxes and delivery** before the order. A clear "order with obligation to pay" button.
- [ ] **Discount reference price:** EU lowest price of the last 30 days; TR lowest of the last 10 days (goods).
- [ ] **Reviews:** verified buyers only, explain how you check, no suppression of negatives.
- [ ] **EU Accessibility Act:** WCAG 2.1 AA + accessibility statement unless you are a microenterprise.
- [ ] **Cards** handled only by a PCI-compliant provider.
- [ ] **Remove** any EU ODR platform link.

## Marketing
- [ ] Marketing email/SMS needs prior opt-in (TR: İYS) and an unsubscribe in every message.
- [ ] Influencer posts labelled as ads; AI use in ads disclosed (TR since 1 Aug 2026).
